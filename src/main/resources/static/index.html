Your Files. Your Privacy. Our Priority.

Your files are processed securely and temporarily. We do not permanently store or share your uploaded files. Temporary files are automatically deleted after processing.

  • Every file is checked for its type, size and real content before it is processed.
  • Files are handled in a private temporary folder under a safe, generated file name.
  • The folder is deleted as soon as processing ends, even when an error happens. A cleanup job also removes any leftovers.
  • There is no database and no account, so nothing about your files is kept after you download the result.
